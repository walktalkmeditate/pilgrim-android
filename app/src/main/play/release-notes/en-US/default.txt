• Honor, the third way to walk: follow a path someone else laid down
• Walk one of your own walks again, a walk someone shared with you, or a stage of the Camino, the Kumano Kodō or Shikoku
• Voices arrive where they were spoken; water and shelter sit quietly on the map
• Save a pilgrimage's maps before you go, and the path is there with no signal
• Walk well.
