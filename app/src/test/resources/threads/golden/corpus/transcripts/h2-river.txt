The weather turned bright and quiet after several cloudy days. I found myself thinking about an old friend I had not spoken to in a long while. Somewhere past the bend the river widened and slowed almost to stillness. I stood at the bank and watched the river catch the afternoon light in long streaks. My legs felt tired but my mind grew strangely clear. I could hear birds calling from somewhere in the reeds and I could smell rain somewhere far off. A dog barked once from a yard nearby and then silence returned. I realized I had been holding my shoulders tight the entire time without noticing. Something about the water always settles my breathing.
