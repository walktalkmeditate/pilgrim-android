A light rain had passed through overnight and everything smelled fresh and green. My phone buzzed twice but I left it in my pocket without looking. I crossed an old stone bridge near the edge of town and stopped halfway to look down at the current below. Ducks paddled lazily near the pilings while a heron watched from the reeds without moving. Something about standing on the bridge made my chest loosen a little, like a held breath finally let go. I could hear traffic somewhere behind me, distant and unbothered. My sister called last night with news I am still turning over slowly. Every quiet minute out here feels like a small gift I did not expect to receive today.
