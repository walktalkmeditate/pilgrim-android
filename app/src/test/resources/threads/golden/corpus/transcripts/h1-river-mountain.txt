This morning I walked out early and the air felt cool against my face. I noticed the light change slowly as the sun climbed higher above some trees. I thought about my job and the choices ahead of me. The river ran quiet beside the trail and I paused to watch the water move past smooth stones. Further along a mountain rose in the distance framed by clouds and for a moment everything else seemed small. I could see the mountain clearly from a ridge and the river curved back toward it. I could imagine standing at the peak someday. My uncle always said patience matters more than speed. I never expected to feel so calm out here alone with my own thoughts drifting quietly onward.
