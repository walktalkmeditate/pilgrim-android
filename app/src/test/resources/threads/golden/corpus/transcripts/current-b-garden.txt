I stopped where the old garden meets the fence and just stood there for a while. I could have kept going but something made me stop. I could see every overgrown bed from where I stood, tangled and quiet. I could have pulled a few weeds but I did not feel like it today. I could hear bees somewhere close by, working without hurry. I could smell wet soil and crushed mint under my shoes. I could have called my brother right then but I put it off again. I could picture the garden the way it looked years ago, tidier, more cared for. I could almost hear my grandmother humming the way she used to out here, always in the same corner. I could stay longer or I could turn back now, and for a long moment I could not decide either way.
