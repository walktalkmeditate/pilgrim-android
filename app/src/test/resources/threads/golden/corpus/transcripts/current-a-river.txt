The morning seemed different today, sharper somehow, like the whole valley had been rinsed overnight. I moved quickly without really deciding to, my legs eager for once. Near the old crossing the river caught the early light and threw it back in broken pieces across the surface. I stopped only briefly, just long enough to watch a heron lift off from the far bank. Further on the river bent away from the open field and disappeared behind a stand of birch. My chest felt still and my breathing came easy the rest of the way. I never once looked at my phone the entire time, which surprised me a little.
