Clouds moved fast overhead and the temperature dropped as the afternoon wore on. I passed a small pond where ducks drifted without much urgency. My thoughts kept circling back to a conversation from last week that still bothered me. Ahead the mountain looked sharper than usual, its edges cut clean against the gray sky. I stopped to catch my breath and studied the mountain for a long while, trying to guess how long the climb would take from here. A church bell rang faintly somewhere in the valley below. I could feel the cold settling into my hands. Every one of these days teaches me something different about patience and about myself.
